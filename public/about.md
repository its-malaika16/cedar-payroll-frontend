# About Cedar Payroll

Cedar Payroll Limited builds payroll software and bureau services so businesses can pay people accurately and on time.

## Mission

Make payroll simple, compliant, and reliable for employers and bureaux in the UK and the regions we serve.

## Vision

A single platform for pay, HMRC, leave, and employee self-service, on the web and on mobile.
